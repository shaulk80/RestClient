    Deviations from plan and open points:
    - Domain body and auth types are all defined now (plan section 4). The builder returns a
      Validation error for form, multipart, and non-none auth until M4 and M5 implement them.
    - VariableResolver takes a plain dictionary. Environments and secrets arrive in M5.
    - Timeout is a fixed 30 s default (RequestBuilder.DefaultTimeout). Settings come in M6.
    - Redirects are fixed at follow, up to 10, in DI (HttpTransportOptions). Settings come in M6.
    - Each send creates its own HttpClient so the connect callback always sees a new connection.
      Connection reuse is not in M1.
    - Spill threshold is 1 MB, not 5 MB (decided 2026-10-24). Bodies up to 1 MB are kept in
      memory in full. Larger bodies are written to a file, and the preview is their first 1 MB.
      Plan section 9 and architecture Q9 still say 5 MB and need updating.
    - Spilled bodies go to data/temp/ and are not cleaned up yet.
    - UI has a Content-Type box, which the plan does not list. Headers and query params are
      supported by RequestBuilder but have no editor in M1.
    - RequestSender does not record history (M3).
